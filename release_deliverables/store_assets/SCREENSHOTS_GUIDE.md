# Google Play Store — Screenshots Capture Guide for Daria

Google Play requires at least **4 phone screenshots** (up to 8 recommended) to submit your store listing. 

To ensure your submission passes review quickly, follow this guide to capture clean, high-resolution screenshots directly from Daria.

---

## 1. Google Play Requirements & Specifications

- **Format:** PNG or JPEG
- **Color space:** RGB
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16 (Standard phone portrait)
- **Recommended Resolutions:**
  - `1080 x 2400` pixels (standard 20:9 Android phone)
  - `1080 x 1920` pixels (standard 16:9 Android phone)
- **Minimum Dimensions:** 320 px (Google requires minimum dimension to be between 320px and 3840px)
- **Rule:** Screenshots must showcase the actual application interface and genuine functionality without misleading graphics or fake devices.

---

## 2. Recommended 7-Screenshot Sequence

Capture these exact 7 screens in portrait mode to highlight Daria's value proposition:

### Screenshot 1: Home Screen (The Visual Photo Calendar)
- **Screen:** Home Screen (`/home`)
- **Setup:** Fill 5–10 days in the current month with clear, colorful daily photos.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Monthly photo thumbnail grid, current streak badge at the top, bottom navigation dock.
- **Caption Suggestion:** *"Your Month at a Glance — Every Day in Photos"*

### Screenshot 2: Today Screen (Capture & Reflection)
- **Screen:** Today Screen (`/today`)
- **Setup:** Today's selected photo in the large viewing card with a thoughtful reflection note entered below (e.g., *"Golden hour walk through the city park. Crisp autumn air and quiet thoughts."*).
- **Key Visual Elements:** Large photo preview, reflection note card, capture & camera action buttons.
- **Caption Suggestion:** *"Capture Today's Memory & Daily Reflection"*

### Screenshot 3: Habit Streak & Progress Card
- **Screen:** Home Streak Card / Streak View
- **Setup:** Active streak count (e.g., 7 or 14 days) showing the radiant burning flame icon, the all-caps `CURRENT STREAK` label, and the 7-day weekly capsules (`M T W T F S S`) with bright red checkmarks.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Glowing flame, bold streak count, weekly habit capsules.
- **Caption Suggestion:** *"Build a Daily Habit with Habit Streaks"*

### Screenshot 4: Memories Timeline
- **Screen:** Memories / Timeline View
- **Setup:** A vertical scrolling feed of past photo entries arranged chronologically with date headers and reflection snippets.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Multi-day feed, photo previews, date stamps.
- **Caption Suggestion:** *"Relive Your Journey Across Time"*

### Screenshot 5: Full Calendar Grid
- **Screen:** Full Calendar Screen
- **Setup:** Full year / multi-month calendar overview showing which days have memories recorded.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Clean calendar matrix, month switcher, floating navigation dock.
- **Caption Suggestion:** *"Browse Your Complete Archive Anytime"*

### Screenshot 6: Custom Themes Showcase
- **Screen:** Theme Selection in Settings
- **Setup:** Display the theme picker showing Daria Red, Nordic Frost, Emerald Grove, and Minimal Slate.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Theme preview cards and color palettes.
- **Caption Suggestion:** *"Personalize Your Diary with Custom Themes"*

### Screenshot 7: 100% Offline Privacy & Safe Backups
- **Screen:** Settings Screen (`/settings`)
- **Setup:** Show the "Export Backup (.padbackup)" and "Export PDF Diary" options.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Clean settings cards, backup format badge, privacy assurances.
- **Caption Suggestion:** *"100% Private, Local-First Storage & Easy PDF Export"*

---

## 3. Best Practices Before Capturing

1. **Clean Status Bar:** Use Android's "Demo Mode" in Developer Options (shows 100% battery, full Wi-Fi/cellular bars, and clean 12:00 or 9:00 time) or crop the system status bar cleanly.
2. **High-Quality Sample Photos:** Use bright, clean, real photos (nature, architecture, coffee, travel, pets) rather than blank or test images.
3. **Consistent Theme:** Capture all primary screenshots in the default **Daria Red** theme for a cohesive, branded store appearance.
