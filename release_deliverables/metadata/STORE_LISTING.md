## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 Characters)

```text
Welcome to Daria — your personal, private, and distraction-free daily photo diary.

Life moves fast, and memories slip away quietly. Daria helps you slow down and preserve what matters most: one snapshot, one note, and one day at a time. Designed with care for privacy and simplicity, Daria is a local-first diary where your personal moments stay exclusively on your device.

CAPTURE AND PRESERVE EVERY DAY
• Daily Snapshot: Take a quick photo using the built-in camera or pick a cherished memory from your gallery.
• Multiple Photos per Day: Some days hold more than one memory. Attach multiple photos to any calendar date.
• Daily Reflections: Pair your pictures with meaningful thoughts, highlights, or journal entries.

EXPLORE YOUR MEMORIES
• Calendar Grid: Browse your months and years through an intuitive visual photo calendar. Tap any date to revisit past memories.
• Memories Timeline: Scroll seamlessly through your timeline of captured moments and reflections.
• Jump to Any Date: Effortlessly navigate to past days, birthdays, anniversaries, and milestones.

STAY MOTIVATED WITH STREAKS & STATS
• Real-time Daily Streaks: Build a consistent habit with streak tracking and visual weekly completion indicators.
• Diary Statistics: Track total photos captured, active months, longest streaks, and completion rates.

LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY & SECURITY
• 100% Offline by Design: Your photos and diary notes are stored directly on your phone's local storage.
• No Cloud Tracking or Ads: No account creation required. No hidden analytics, no ad tracking, and no external data brokers.
• Safe Backups: Export your complete diary into a secure, portable `.padbackup` archive and restore it anytime.
• PDF Export: Generate elegant, high-resolution PDF journals of your diary to print, archive, or share with loved ones.

PERSONALIZED DESIGN
• Beautiful Themes: Customize your experience with multiple visual themes, including Daria Red, Nordic Frost, Emerald Grove, Sunset Terracotta, and Minimal Slate.
• Clean, Modern Interface: Distraction-free navigation designed to put your photos and memories front and center.

Start building your personal visual story today with Daria.
```
