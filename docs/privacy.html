Personal Daily Photo Diary — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 Last Updated: November 1, 2026 Package: com.photoaday.photo_per_day

1. Introduction

Daria ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal daily photo diary designed with a strict local-first, privacy-by-design philosophy. We believe your private memories, photos, reflections, and thoughts belong exclusively to you.

This Privacy Policy explains how Daria handles your information when you use our mobile and desktop applications.

2. Summary of Key Principles

🔒 Zero Cloud Storage
All photos, notes, calendar entries, and metadata are stored exclusively on your device's local storage.
👤 No Account Required
No registration, login, email address, phone number, or credentials are ever required to use Daria.
🚫 No Tracking or Analytics
Zero tracking SDKs, zero analytics tools, zero crash log uploaders, and zero third-party advertising frameworks.
🛡️ No Data Monetization
We never collect, sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information or media.

3. Information We Access and How It Is Used

Daria requests device permissions solely to provide its core features:

A. Camera android.permission.CAMERA

Purpose: Used strictly when you choose to take a photo within the app to record your daily memory.

Data Handling: Camera images are saved directly to your device's private application storage. They are never transmitted to external servers.

B. Device Storage / Photo Library READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (API ≤ 32) / Photo Picker (API 33+)

Purpose: Used only when you choose to import an existing photo from your gallery into your diary.

Data Handling: Only the specific photo(s) you select are copied to your local diary directory. Daria never scans, indexes, or reads other files on your device.

C. Notifications android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Purpose: Used if you choose to enable daily reminder notifications to prompt you to take your daily photo.

Data Handling: All reminders are scheduled locally on your device via Android's local alarm system. No push notification servers or cloud messaging tokens are used.

D. Internet Access & Voluntary External Feedback android.permission.INTERNET

Purpose: Used solely when you voluntarily interact with external links (such as submitting feedback or reporting a bug).

Data Handling: Daria does not maintain a backend server and performs no background telemetry. If you tap "Report a Bug", "Suggest a Feature", or "General Feedback" in Settings, Daria opens an external web form (hosted on Tally.so) in your device's default web browser.

ℹ️ Note Regarding External Feedback (Tally.so)

Feedback submissions are 100% voluntary. When you submit feedback via the external Tally form in your web browser, any information you provide (such as feedback comments or an optional email address for follow-up) is handled directly by Tally in accordance with Tally's Privacy Policy. Daria never attaches device identifiers, diary entries, or private photos to feedback submissions.

4. Data Storage and Backup

  • Local Application Storage: All diary data, including original photos, thumbnails, and metadata, resides in your device's private application sandbox.
  • Backups (.padbackup): When you export a backup, Daria packages your diary into a standalone .padbackup archive file saved to a location you choose. You maintain complete custody and control over this file.
  • PDF Export: If you export a PDF diary, the document is generated entirely on your device and shared using your operating system's native share sheet.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Daria does not store your diary data on external servers:

  • You can delete any individual photo or note directly within the app at any time.
  • You can permanently erase all diary data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.
  • Once deleted locally, your data cannot be recovered by us, as we do not keep remote backups.

6. Children's Privacy

Daria is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted to this public page and reflected with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the in-app Feedback & Support form or submit feedback directly via our feedback channel.
